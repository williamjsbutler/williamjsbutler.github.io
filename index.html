MECHANICAL DESIGN / CAD

Fusion Reactor Vacuum Chamber

Designed and prototyped the central vacuum chamber for a fusor device. The project includes a machined hemispherical chamber, flanges, and vacuum fittings shown here in a complete assembly developed in Fusion 360.

The design process included CAD modeling, collaborative design iterations, engineering drawings, and design modifications for component integration.

Fusion 360 CAD Mechanical Design Engineering Drawings
Section View Front sectional view of fusor chamber showing contact points between both steel hemispheres and attached nipples. Designed to be connected with interior welds to create airtight seals and allow for instrument implementation.
Detail View Sectional view of assembled chamber model highlighting the altered flanges fitted around the rim of each hemisphere allowing for a flush airtight connection between the flange and hemisphere, as well as both halves of the full chamber.
